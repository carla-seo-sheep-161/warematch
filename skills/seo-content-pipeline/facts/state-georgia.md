## A: Georgia DOR
- Freeport, dor.georgia.gov/node/22401 (no date; O.C.G.A. 48-5-48.1, 48-5-48.2): a county or municipality "may elect, with the approval of the voters" to adopt it, at 20, 40, 60, 80 or 100 percent of inventory value. Level 1 includes "Inventory of finished goods on January 1 that are stored in a warehouse, dock, or wharf" "which are destined for shipment outside this State for a period not to exceed 12 months" and "Stock in trade of a fulfillment center which on January 1 are stored in the fulfillment center." Level 2: "goods, wares, and merchandise of every character and kind." Apply to the county Board of Tax Assessors. The page does not say how goods held by a third party for an out-of-state owner are treated: make no claim; no county rates checked.
- Remote sellers, Policy Bulletin SUT-2019-02, May 7, 2019 (dor.georgia.gov/media/35301/download): a remote seller sells "from outside of Georgia" to a Georgia delivery location, with a gross-revenue OR sales-count threshold in the "previous or current calendar year." Dollar and count figures did not extract (UNVERIFIED; third-party $100,000 not used). Neither this bulletin nor dor.georgia.gov/taxes/sales-use-tax/out-state-sellers mentions inventory or warehouses: nexus from stock at a Georgia 3PL is UNVERIFIED; refer to a tax adviser.
