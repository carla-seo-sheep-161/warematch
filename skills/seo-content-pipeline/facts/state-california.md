## A (V): Operating rules
- AB 701 (Labor Commissioner FAQ, no date; https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/FAQ_warehousequotas.htm): "The law covers employers who directly or indirectly control 100 or more employees at a single warehouse distribution center or 1,000 or more employees at one or more warehouse distribution centers in California." Staffing-agency employees "may also be included". Effective Jan 1, 2022; written quota description required (Lab. Code 2101); no quota that prevents meal or rest periods or bathroom use (2102). Do not say it applies to a given site.
- CDTFA (https://cdtfa.ca.gov/industry/fulfillment-centers/; only date is a June 2025 accessibility certification): fulfillment center that "only provides this service for the owners of the goods and does not sell goods" is "not required to register" with CDTFA. "If you are an out-of-state seller that uses a California fulfillment center to store your inventory for delivery to consumers in California, you are also required to register with CDTFA and file sales and use tax returns." Also: such a seller "is considered to be engaged in business in California." Consignment and drop-ship arrangements can make the center the retailer. Page states the exact case; add "confirm with CDTFA or a tax adviser".
- CARB ACF (https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/our-work/programs/advanced-clean-fleets): "requires State and Local Government Agency fleets to reduce emissions by increasing the use of Zero-Emission Vehicles". Rulemaking page (https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/rulemaking/2025/acfab1594): "OAL has approved this rulemaking action and filed with the Secretary of State on September 10, 2026". Guidance (https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/resources/fact-sheets/ACF-2025-Amendments-Guidance): amendments "Repeal the High Priority Fleets and Drayage portions". So private drayage and high-priority fleet purchase rules are repealed per CARB; other CARB truck rules not checked (U).
- Prop 65: OEHHA blocks automated reads; only a search snippet (U). Do not use.
