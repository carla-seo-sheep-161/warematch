## A — Compliance, re-read at the source by me on 2026-10-21 (full agent notes: research/compliance_us.md)
- California CDTFA (https://cdtfa.ca.gov/industry/fulfillment-centers/; page shows 2025 accessibility dates): a seller outside California that stores inventory in a California fulfillment center "is considered to be engaged in business in California" and so must register with CDTFA, file returns and pay tax on sales to California consumers.
- Texas Comptroller Pub. 94-108 (12/2020; https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/publications/94-108.php): a location in Texas that you operate directly or through an agent; examples include a distribution center, warehouse, storage place.
- New York TSB-A-24(52)S (Nov 12, 2024; https://www.tax.ny.gov/pubs_and_bulls/advisory_opinions/sales/24-52s.htm): property on the premises of an unaffiliated fulfillment center "does not create nexus for the seller if the services provided ... are limited to" order acceptance, consumer correspondence, billing/collection, or shipment from inventory; the opinion "is limited to the facts set forth therein and is binding on the Department only with respect to the person or entity to whom it is issued."
- CBP bonded warehouse (https://www.cbp.gov/document/publications/us-customs-and-border-protection-bonded-warehouses; Last Modified Aug 12, 2026): "A Customs bonded warehouse is a building or other secured area in which imported dutiable merchandise may be stored, manipulated, or undergo manufacturing operations without payment of duty for up to 5 years from the date of importation."
- trade.gov FTZ (https://www.trade.gov/about-ftzs, no date): "A foreign-trade zone is a designated location in the United States where companies can use special customs procedures"; benefits listed: duties and federal excise tax deferred on imports; no duties on imported goods later re-exported.
- FDA food facility registration, 21 CFR 1.225 (read via Cornell LII mirror of the CFR; eCFR link: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/part-1/section-1.225): you must register if you are the owner, operator or agent in charge of a facility engaged in manufacturing/processing, packing, or holding food for consumption in the U.S., unless exempt (1.226).
- FDA cosmetics (https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/registration-listing-cosmetic-product-facilities-and-products, current as of 09/09/2026): manufacturers and processors must register facilities and renew every two years; a responsible person (manufacturer, packer or distributor whose name is on the label) must list each marketed cosmetic product. DO NOT state any exemption for warehouses that only hold cosmetics (the page does not say it; agent cited guidance, unverified by me).
- 49 CFR 173.185 (read via Cornell LII; eCFR https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/part-173/section-173.185): each lithium cell or battery must be of the type proven to meet the criteria in part III, sub-section 38.3 of the UN Manual of Tests and Criteria.
