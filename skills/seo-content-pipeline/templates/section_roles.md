# Section role contract: state template (and the country template's two sections)
The three authored sections are stages of ONE buyer journey. Each answers a different reader question and produces a different output. If a paragraph could be moved to another of the three without anyone noticing, it is in the wrong place or should be cut.

| Template section (heading may be reworded; it must stay recognizable) | Reader question | Output the reader leaves with | Content allowed | Content NOT allowed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **When to Choose a 3PL in [State]** (table: 'You need a [State] 3PL if' | 'You may not need one yet if') | WHETHER: "Should I outsource at all, and is it the right time?" | A verdict: tests about the SHIPPER's own situation, each tied to a real state condition (freight entry point, customer geography, perishables, storm exposure, volume). 4+ bullets per column. | Provider advice, 'ask each provider', 'filter', figures (unless sourced), thresholds nobody can source. |
| **What to Consider When Choosing a 3PL in [State]** (3 points) | WHERE: "Given I will outsource, what does this state's landscape mean for where my stock should sit?" | A location plan: which market/node, what hazards and gateways shape the choice. | State-level FACTS (cited, rounded, dated) + the implication for choosing a site: market/workforce geography, hazards (climate, flood), freight gateways (ports, airports, interstates, borders). Facts-heavy, comparative, one so-what per fact. | Questions for providers, red flags, 'good answers', contract or tax clauses (those are vetting). |
| **What to Look for in a [State] 3PL** (3 points; second section, different aspect) | WHICH/HOW: "How do I vet and contract with a provider here?" | A checklist to use on calls and in the RFQ. | Provider-facing ASKS + good answer / red flag per point: certifications and capabilities tied to the state's conditions, continuity and risk terms, tax/rent and contract terms; WareMatch actions (capability filter, city filter, one request listing the fields providers need). | Restating the state facts already given above (point back or add the provider-facing layer only). |
Rules: (1) A theme (e.g. hurricane season) may appear in all three only as three different layers: trigger (decide) -> cited fact + location implication (landscape) -> provider ask + red flag (vet). (2) Each section must teach something the other two do not; test: delete it, does the reader lose a decision tool? (3) Landscape points carry the cited data; vet points carry the asks; decide bullets carry no data. (4) WareMatch connects in the vet section (filters, one request, compare matched providers) and once in the TL;DR; never an invented network count. (5) Applies to every state page and to the country template's 'What to Consider' / 'What to Look for'. Enforced heuristically by geo-intent-audit 3d (section roles + overlap); the human read-through confirms.
