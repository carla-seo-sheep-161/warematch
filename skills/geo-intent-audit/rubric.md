# Judged rubric (score 0-5; quote the page; state the evidence grade A/B/C/D)
| # | Dimension | What to judge | Grade / source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intent fit | Dominant SERP intent and content type (NW serp_summary); does the first screen do that job? Are the top query clusters answered (audit.py coverage)? | A: Google helpful-content guidance |
| 2 | Non-commodity information gain | List what the top results cover that this page lacks, and what this page has that none do. Does it contain unique expert/experienced takes beyond common knowledge, local or cited facts? 1 = restates common knowledge. | A: Google AI optimization guide (2026-07-10), references/content_evidence.md |
| 3 | Decision utility | Thresholds, if/then rules, red flags, worked examples with stated assumptions; would a reader choose or act differently? | analogy (A for helpfulness) |
| 4 | Specificity / swap test | Replace the product or place name: does the section still read the same? Count named rules, datasets, figures with period. | analogy |
| 5 | Answer-first, self-contained passages | Direct 40-80 word answers under descriptive headings; no "this/it" openers; terms defined once; comprehensive coverage; neutral tone. | C: AutoGEO (+36% avg visibility in its benchmark); NOT required by Google |
| 6 | Sourced, dated statistics; real attributed quotes | Publisher + period in the same sentence; quotes named and real. Quality practice, not a lift guarantee. | C mixed: GEO paper positive, C-SEO Bench found statistics lowered rank in most settings |
| 7 | Fan-out coverage | Sub-questions a searcher/AI would branch to are answered (use GSC conversational queries). | A (Google describes query fan-out); effect C |
| 8 | Eligibility and visibility | Indexed, snippet-eligible, not blocked for search bots, key content in raw HTML (not only tabs, accordions, PDFs, images or "view all" clicks). | A: Google AI features page (2025-12-10); JS for non-Google bots = prior from a 2024 third-party study (B) |
| 9 | Structured data hygiene | Valid types only, visible-content-only, no fake ratings, correct breadcrumb URLs, one tidy graph. NOT a citation lever. | A for rules; B: Ahrefs causal test found no measurable AI lift (2026-05) |
| 10 | Freshness | Visible, truthful "updated" date where the content changes; never fake. Modest weight. | B mixed (Ahrefs 2025 assistants prefer fresher; AI Overviews showed none) |
| 11 | Trust and entity consistency | Primary sources, hedged narrow claims, no unsourced marketing claims (check template copy separately), consistent names/facts site-wide. | A (Google people-first guidance) |
| 12 | Locality (location pages) | Swap-test the place name; list which facts change. Almost none = boilerplate. | A (thin templated location pages are a flagged quality risk) |
| 13 | House rules | Template lock, metadata rules (title <=65, description <=160, "3PL", brand suffix, CTA), no competitor names/rankings, no emoji. | team docs |
| 14 | Negative flags | Keyword stuffing, hype words, templated filler, fake dates. | C: GEO paper (stuffing no gain); Google spam policies |
